CloudDocs Help Center
=====================

Q: How do I upload a file?
A: Click the "Upload" button in the top-right corner, then drag and drop your files.

Q: What is the maximum file size?
A: Free plan: 50MB per file. Pro plan: 5GB per file.

Q: How do I share a file?
A: Right-click any file and select "Share". You can share via link or email.

Q: Can I recover deleted files?
A: Yes! Deleted files stay in Trash for 30 days. Go to Trash and click "Restore".
